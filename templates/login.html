Example custom app · CTM softphone embedded

Your phone system, anywhere your software lives.

Call software usually asks your team to leave the tools they work in. This is the other pattern: the CTM softphone lives inside the app — the CRM record, the patient chart, the operations dashboard — so talking to a customer happens right next to everything you already know about them. Calls, texts, form submissions, and agent presence render inside your own UI, on your own terms.

Built to sit inside your app Drop the CTM phone beside any record. A few lines of markup and two token endpoints on your server — CTM handles the rest.
What shows up Live softphone, call & text history, form submissions, agent availability, and activity feeds — all in one workspace.

Authorization is OAuth — the client secret stays on your server, never in the browser.

How to connect

Three steps, about a minute.

  1. 1 Click Connect with CTM and sign in to your CTM account. If you're not already signed in to CTM, sign in there first, then come back and click the button.
  2. 2 Approve access on CTM's authorize screen.
  3. 3 You land straight in the live workspace — no keys to copy.
